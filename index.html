Our Daily Baking Rhythm

While the neighborhood sleeps, our bakers are keeping the old traditions alive by hand. Here is how a simple flour sack becomes our signature wild sourdough loaf.

Read Our Full Baking Philosophy
01

Milling & Mixing

We blend organic, regionally sourced heritage flours with high hydration spring water to preserve natural wheat nutrients.

02

Wild Culturing

No commercial yeast. We feed our century-old starter daily, using slow ambient cooling to build custom depth of flavor.

03

Hearth-Baking

Our hand-shaped loaves are loaded into deep stone deck ovens heated to scorching temperatures to lock in interior moisture.

04

The Sunrise Crackle

The best sound in the world is fresh bread singing and crackling on wood racks as cold morning air hits the oven-warm crust.
