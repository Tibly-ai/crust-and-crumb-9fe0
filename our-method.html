Our Daily Ritual

The 36-Hour Sourdough Process

Every loaf starts as stone-ground heritage flour and finishes its journey in our hot stone deck oven. Here is how we transform four simple ingredients into bread that nourishes.

01

Heritage Grain Blend

We source custom stone-milled flours directly from regional growers who prioritize soil health and native heirloom grains. This preserves the wheat's natural bran and germ for rich, earthy flavor.

02

Autolyse & Mix

Flour and pure water rest together before mixing. This gentle, pre-ferment hydration period allows proteins to relax, creating a silky gluten network and an extremely tender, open crumb.

03

Cold Retardation

Once shaped, the dough cures in cold storage for up to 24 hours. The cold temperature slows yeast activity while friendly bacteria produce organic acids, giving our bread its distinctive sour note.

04

Deck Oven Bake

Loaded directly onto our heavy stone hearth decks with generous steam. The high initial heat creates rapid oven spring, opening the signature scored ear and forming a blistered, golden crust.
