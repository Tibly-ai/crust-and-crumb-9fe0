Your Morning Ritual

Step into the warmth of our micro-bakery.

The heavy wooden door opens to the rich aroma of stone-ground flour, sweet butter, and rich espresso. Every morning, we pull dark, blistered sourdough loaves from our stone-deck hearth at dawn.

We operate as a small-batch kitchen. Because we focus on slow fermentation and hand-shaped bakes, our daily selections are limited. We highly recommend arriving early while the shelves are full and the crusts are still warm.
